## 💡 About AgroNear

**AgroNear** is an Android application designed to connect farmers directly with buyers through a simple, secure, and transparent digital marketplace.

🚜 The platform removes intermediaries and empowers farmers to sell their agricultural products directly to customers.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Farmers often struggle to reach buyers directly and depend on middlemen, resulting in:

- ❌ Unfair pricing  
- ❌ Reduced profits  
- ❌ Limited market access  

AgroNear bridges this gap by providing a **direct digital marketplace** accessible through mobile devices.

---

## ✨ Key Features ⚡

- 👤 Farmer & Buyer Registration and Login  
- 🧑‍🌾 Product upload with image and pricing  
- 🛒 Browse agricultural products  
- ❤️ Wishlist functionality  
- 🔐 JWT-based authentication  
- ⚡ Fast & responsive modern UI

## ⚙️ How It Works 🧭

1️⃣ User registers or logs in as **Farmer** or **Buyer**  
2️⃣ Farmers upload product details with image and price  
3️⃣ Buyers browse available products  
4️⃣ Buyers can add products to wishlist  
5️⃣ Secure communication with backend APIs using JWT
